Emoo-Boy

My Game Boy emulator, written in C and compiled to WebAssembly so it runs right here.

About

Your ROM is read in your browser and never uploaded. For games that save, use Download save to keep your progress.

Original Game Boy games only, and it needs a keyboard. Use Change ROM to switch games.
